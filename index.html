SeraphIRC

An IRC client for Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android. Connect straight to a network or through your Soju or ZNC bouncer. It has classic and modern layouts and broad IRCv3 support.

Features

  • Soju and ZNC

    Import and manage your Soju networks from inside the client. Push notifications use soju.im/webpush. ZNC works too, including command forwarding.

  • Modern and Classic layouts

    Modern groups a run of messages under one name and avatar. Classic is the plain [11:15] <@nick> transcript.

  • 22 built-in themes

    Try them with the picker at the top of this page. Clone one in the theme editor to make your own. Share it or download others on the theme marketplace.

  • Seraph Transfer and Seraph Chat

    Send a file or open a private conversation straight to another SeraphIRC user over mutually authenticated TLS 1.3. It works when you're both behind NAT and nothing is stored on a server.

  • Send Guard

    Asks first when a message looks like a mistake, like a password typed into a channel or a 40-line paste.

  • Nothing phones home

    You don't need an account and there's no cloud service. The client only talks to your networks.

Connecting

  • All your networks in one tree Direct networks and bouncer networks sit side by side.
  • Direct connections Works fine without a bouncer too.
  • IRCv3 SASL, chat history, away-notify and plenty more. Full list below.
  • Connection diagnostics See what went wrong when a connection or login fails.

    It shows what the server sent back and what the client thinks is going on.

Chatting

  • Replies and reactions Answer a specific message or react to it with an emoji.

    If the network can't show them, replies fall back to a plain nick: prefix and reactions can be sent as normal messages.

  • Markdown and color Type markdown or tokens for bold, italics, code and colors.

    Other clients see normal IRC formatting.

  • Custom mentions Add your own words and phrases and they highlight like your nick. Desktop only.

    They get their own tray and notification toggle.

  • Verified logins The query window shows whether someone logged in with SASL. A green check means verified.

    It also shows which account they logged in to.

  • Emoji and GIFs An emoji picker, plus GIPHY search with your own API key.
  • Message redaction Take back a message you sent, on networks that support it.

Files and privacy

  • Uploads and DCC Upload files through your Soju bouncer, or send them directly with DCC.

    Drag and drop and copy/paste both work.

  • FiSH encryption Set a shared key for a channel or a person and messages are encrypted automatically.

    Blowfish in CBC or ECB mode, compatible with HexChat, Konversation, Quassel, and mIRC.

Looks

  • Atmosphere A slider for the clouds and stars behind the desktop client.
  • Fonts and spacing Pick your interface and message fonts, icon size and how tightly things are packed.

On iOS and Android

Connect through your Soju or ZNC bouncer, or use Direct IRC on networks like Ergo that keep you connected on their own.

Push notifications can be set per network or per bouncer.

It's laid out like the desktop app, so you already know your way around.
