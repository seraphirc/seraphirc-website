SeraphIRC

An IRC client for Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone and iPad. Connect straight to a network or through your Soju or ZNC bouncer. It has classic and modern layouts and broad IRCv3 support.

Features

Connecting

All your networks in one tree
Direct networks, bouncer imports, channels, server buffers, and private messages all sit in the same tree.
Soju
Import networks from Soju and manage bouncer-aware connections inside the client, including push notifications over soju.im/webpush.
ZNC
Existing setups keep working, including command forwarding and the usual user/network style login.
Direct connections
No bouncer? Connect straight to a network and everything works the way you expect.
IRCv3
SASL, server-time, account tags, away-notify, echo-message, batch, chat history and more, used wherever the network offers them. Full list below.
Diagnostics built in
When a connection or a login goes wrong, you can watch what the connection is doing, what the server sent back, and what the client thinks is going on.

Files and privacy

Uploads and DCC
Upload files through your Soju bouncer, or send them directly with DCC. Drag and drop and copy/paste both work.
Seraph Transfer and Seraph Chat
Send a file or open a private conversation straight to another SeraphIRC user over mutually authenticated TLS 1.3. It works when you are both behind NAT, and nothing is stored on a server.
FiSH encryption
Blowfish in CBC or ECB mode, compatible with HexChat, Konversation, Quassel, and mIRC. Set a shared key for a channel or a person and messages are encrypted automatically.
Nothing phones home
No account requirement and no cloud dependency. The client talks to your networks and nothing else.

Chatting

Modern and Classic layouts
Modern groups a run of messages under one name and avatar. Classic is the plain [11:15] <@nick> transcript.
Replies and reactions
Answer a specific message or react to it with an emoji. Where a network or client can't show them, replies fall back to a plain nick: prefix and reactions can go out as a normal message.
Markdown and color
Write bold, italics, code spans, and colors with markdown or token syntax. It all goes out as standard IRC formatting codes that other clients understand.
Send Guard
Holds a message that looks like an accident, such as a password typed into a channel or a forty-line paste, and asks before it goes out.
Custom mentions
Add your own words and phrases and they highlight like your nick, with their own tray and notification toggle. Desktop only.
Know who you're talking to
The query window shows whether someone logged in with SASL, and to which account. A green check means verified.
Emoji and GIFs
A full emoji picker, plus GIPHY search if you bring your own API key.
Message redaction
Take back a message you sent, wherever the network supports it.

Looks

22 built-in themes
Try them on this site with the picker at the top of the page. You can also clone any of them in the built-in editor, and share or download custom themes on the theme marketplace.
Atmosphere
A slider for how much cloud and starlight drifts behind the desktop client.
Your fonts, your spacing
Pick the interface and message fonts, the icon size, and how tightly messages and the user list are packed.

On iPhone and iPad

Connect through your Soju or ZNC bouncer, or use Direct IRC on networks like Ergo that keep you connected on their own.

Push notifications can be set per network or per bouncer.

It's laid out like the desktop app, so you already know your way around.
