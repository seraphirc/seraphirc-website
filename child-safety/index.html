Child Safety Standards for SeraphIRC

Effective date: Wednesday, November 18, 2026

SeraphIRC is an IRC client for desktop and mobile, published by Seraph Technologies LLC. These standards cover child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Zero Tolerance

SeraphIRC prohibits any use of the app to create, share, request, or promote child sexual abuse material, or to groom, sexualize, solicit, traffic, or otherwise exploit a minor. This applies to messages, files, images, links, and nicknames, and to anything shared through services we operate.

How SeraphIRC Works

SeraphIRC connects you to IRC servers and networks that you choose. Those networks are run by independent operators, not by us. We do not host your messages or files. Each network has its own staff, who can remove users and content from their network.

Content on services we operate, such as the theme marketplace on this site, is covered by these standards and is removed when it violates them.

Reporting

If you see CSAE content or behavior:

  • Use Ignore on the user in the app to stop seeing their messages.
  • Report it to the operators of the IRC network where it happened. Most networks list a contact on their website or in a help channel.
  • Report it to us at privacy@seraphirc.chat. Include the network, channel, nickname, and time if you can. We review every report.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, contact local law enforcement. In the US, you can also report to the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org. Outside the US, find your national hotline at inhope.org.

Do not send us copies of images or files. Describe where you saw them instead.

What We Do

  • Remove CSAE content from services we operate and block those responsible.
  • Report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to other authorities as required by law.
  • Pass reports about IRC networks to their operators when we can reach them.
  • Cooperate with law enforcement as required by law.

Age

SeraphIRC is not directed to children under 13.
