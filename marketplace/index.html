Submit your theme

Made something worth showing off? Export it from the desktop client and email the file to themes@seraphirc.chat. Every submission is checked with the client's own theme validators and reviewed by a person. Once approved, it shows up in the list above with your nickname on it.

  • Export the file: Settings > Interface > Appearance, select your theme in the dropdown, then Edit theme > Export.
  • Include your nickname so the theme can be credited to you.
  • Optionally, your preferred network, so others can find you if they want to ask about the theme.
  • Your email address is only used for questions about the submission. It is never used for marketing and never sold or shared.

Questions

Send questions about themes to themes@seraphirc.chat. Want a theme taken down or updated? Same address, and include the theme name.
